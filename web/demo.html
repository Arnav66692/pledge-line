A restart between supplier payments

A lost response
is not another charge.

The accepted purchase survived an app and supplier-lab restart between its two legs. The original browser session recovered the partial order, then completed the second approved leg.

  1. Commit the attempt. Store the exact commercial terms and payment identity before dispatch.
  2. Check the same identity. Read an uncertain payment from PayPal. A missing response does not grant permission to replace it.
  3. Recover the evidence. Approved quotes, review, timeline and structured negotiation trace stay attached to the purchase.

The new structured trace applies to requests made after this upgrade. Earlier accepted records retain their original evidence. Unapproved quote sessions remain in memory. A write without a returned provider identity remains unknown until it can be established safely.
