The after-hours line for subscribersA demo, no real money.

For subscribers

Any subscriber can run this line.

Any business or person with a PayPal subscription can run this line. A gym, a creator, a club, a small shop or a nonprofit can each give its subscribers a voice line that checks, pauses, restarts or cancels a monthly payment, by voice.

The donor line is the first example. A different business changes three settings, listed below, and the line works the same way.

This is a demo on PayPal's test system. No real money moves, and the subscription belongs to a test PayPal account.

Try the donor line

What a subscriber can do by voice

  • Check the gift or subscription. The line reads its amount, status and next payment from PayPal.
  • Pause it. The line asks for a yes before it changes anything.
  • Restart it. A paused gift starts again after a yes.
  • Cancel it. Cancelling ends it for good and cannot be undone, so the line asks for a yes first. It offers a pause before it cancels.
  • Ask how it knows. Say "how do you know". The line reads PayPal again and says what it just checked.

What it does not do

  • It does not start a new monthly subscription. It works on the subscription that already exists. It can send a one-time invoice or, for a sponsor's Covered Month offer, a refund. Both need a spoken yes and a PayPal read-back first.
  • It does not change a plan. It pauses, restarts or cancels a subscription. It does not switch it to another plan.
  • It does not settle disputes. It can read a charge back to you and log a refund request for staff. Staff decide what happens next.

Run it for your business

Three settings make the line yours. Set them as environment variables on the server, then follow the steps in SETUP.md.

  • Its PayPal subscription id. Set as DEMO_SUBSCRIPTION_ID.
  • Its name for the line. Set as ORG_NAME. Digits, @ and $ are not allowed in it.
  • Its PIN. Set as PILOT_PIN. Use 4 to 6 digits. Callers type it on the keypad.
