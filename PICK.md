Pick, Nov 3, second round. Covered Month. A monthly donor calls and says she lost her job and can't do this month. The line reads her last charge from PayPal and checks a staff rule, up to $50, within 30 days, once a year. It offers to give the month back, with a sponsor's hardship fund paying the same amount to the nonprofit. On her spoken yes it captures the same amount from the sponsor's PayPal hold, refunds her charge, and reads both back from PayPal.
Why. Arnav asked for money to actually move. Strict judges scored the entry 61 of 90 against a 72 bar, and the main complaint was that no money moves. Two real sandbox moves now run on one yes, and the sponsor is the part the research stage found no twin for. Judgment, the score guess is 65 to 68, which is still under the 72 bar, so this lifts the entry and does not clear it by itself.
What it rests on. Keepwell's sandbox spike got a 201 refunding a subscription's transaction id through v2 captures, and a replay with the same PayPal-Request-Id returned the same refund (https://github.com/NotArnav03/keepwell/blob/main/docs/spikes/Q1.md, opened Nov 3). AgentTab's sandbox run did partial captures with final_capture false (https://github.com/zuemen/agent-tab/blob/main/docs/SANDBOX_EVIDENCE.md, from the research stage, not reopened here). None of this has run in our own sandbox yet.
Known weak spots. A skeptical judge's first question is who the sponsor is, and no sponsor or nonprofit was found asking for this. The research stage found soft demand for the donor half only, and no sourced harm number for this exact case. The hold is not standing, it lasts 29 days and PayPal advises capturing within 3, so it is pitched as a monthly hold and seeded within 3 days of filming. The sponsor half can read like the crowded escrow cluster and like the earlier loser Match Means Matched, so it is pitched as hardship cover and not matching.
Earlier pick, still in the line. Is this charge real. A donor who sees a PayPal charge she doesn't recognize calls, the line reads the real PayPal transaction and says the amount, the date, the status and the org, and a yes logs a staff refund request instead of a bank dispute. It moves no money, which is why it was not enough on its own.
Runner-up, give less not zero. It lost because Salesforce already ships an AI agent that changes a donor's amount, and the pitched revise call needs the donor to log in again, so the honest build is a PATCH with a 10-day delay the line would have to explain.
Runners-up, skip one month and pause until a date. They lost because donor portals already ship both (Fundraise Up, WinRed, ACLU, Givebutter, GiveWP), PayPal has no resume-at-date, and the scheduled restart is a second write with no live yes, run by a Render service that sleeps.
Runner-up, stop a loved one's gifts. It lost on hours (about 9), a thin want signal for charities, and a new caller role with its own verification that a judge will poke at.
