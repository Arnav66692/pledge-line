## Audience and workflow
Pledge Line serves small nonprofits and donors needing help outside staffed hours. The primary workflow is donor care: read a gift, propose an exact supported action, obtain fresh separate consent, execute it, read PayPal again and produce an inspectable receipt. Eligible Covered Month relief captures sponsor funding before refunding a donor's latest charge; the monthly gift stays active. An unfamiliar-charge review is a different staff request, not an automatic refund. A separate staff purchasing desk uses the same exact approval and payment evidence principles. No assertion that refunds fund purchasing.
