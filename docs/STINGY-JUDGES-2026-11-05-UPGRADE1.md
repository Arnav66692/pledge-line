Three fresh AI reviewers assessed `ceda8e4` and the published laboratory evidence before upgraded hosted acceptance. Scores were **68, 68 and 70/90, median 68**. This is a subjective internal assessment using the same nine criterion names. Official eligibility, selection probability and founder-specific domain access were not established. One reviewer incidentally saw earlier scores in the older acceptance document and disclosed that exposure. The other two reported no prior-score exposure. This is not an external blind panel or official Claude/PayPal test.

Reviewers credited the complete negotiation trace, exact quote binding, grouped price guard, explained Jev uncertainty, anonymous receipt verification, captions and recorded evidence walkthrough. They still deducted for missing observed user benefit, sponsor willingness and economics, production identity, independent suppliers, and unproved AI-specific economic value.

The V1 comparison exposed $39.05 of model overpayment against deterministic negotiation. The corrective V2 guard produced five complete orders at the same $961.32 as the strong baseline, prevented $111.80 of candidate overpayment, and used twice as many negotiations. That is evidence of a working protective guard, not superior AI pricing. Both reports remain intact.

Concrete presentation findings were stale restart-retention text and older current-state documentation. Those are being corrected. The next code improvement is plain-language goal intake, where language interpretation has a distinct purpose. It will be evaluated separately on new frozen instances, not treated as an automatic score increase.

No reviewer established an 80/90 outcome. The user asked to finish the software before conducting a usability session. Missing real-user evidence has not been replaced by invented testimonials, fabricated task results or another favorable review of unchanged code.
