# One nonprofit, one clear demonstration

The operator is a coordinator at the fictional Sunny Hollow Pantry. Donor care is the primary story. Supply desk is a separate staff purchasing task, such as volunteer shirts for a pantry distribution. A supplier quote is a promise, not fulfilled stock. Do not imply that donor refunds finance procurement or that the app links those balances.

## Three-minute recording plan

| Time | Show | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:25 | A donor says they cannot afford this month. Show the exact proposed relief and the verified outcome immediately. | Actual sandbox acceptance before presenting this as an actual provider result. Otherwise visibly label the sequence simulated. |
| 0:25 to 0:45 | Explain the donor's problem and the pantry's proposed hardship fund. | State that the people and pantry are fictional; do not claim observed demand or sponsor participation. |
| 0:45 to 1:35 | Separate consent, sponsor-first funding and the exact donor refund. | Fresh completed sponsor capture, verified donor refund and truthful fee handling. Never describe a partial outcome as a rollback. |
| 1:35 to 2:05 | Ask how the line knows. Open the PayPal-backed evidence and donor receipt. | Exact object IDs, recorded read evidence and a valid receipt signature. |
| 2:05 to 2:30 | One clearly labeled lost-response drill and same-identity recovery. | Distinguish a staged fault from an actual provider outage; show no duplicate dispatch. |
| 2:30 to 2:58 | The coordinator's actionable record, then the implementation and test boundary. | Name actual services used, actual versus simulated flows, current deployment and what remains unvalidated. |

Keep the procurement flow available for judge testing and a separate short walkthrough. If that becomes the primary submission instead, record one complete nonprofit purchase: the brief, initial quote, inspectable agent counteroffer, supplier response, complete-cost allocation, exact approval, PayPal buyer approval, capture and authoritative read-back. Demonstrate one cheap but invalid quote and one uncertain outcome blocking a later supplier. Do not switch between two unrelated primary stories during the same three-minute video.

## Evidence that must stay separate

- Local unit tests and simulated donor scripts.
- Authenticated HTTP MCP transport and controlled supplier provenance.
- Actual Neon persistence, concurrent claims and restart-style workflow restoration.
- Actual Anthropic/Jev inference and the unchanged preregistered evaluation.
- Actual PayPal sandbox buyer approval, capture and authoritative reads.
- Independent supplier participation, real user observations and fulfilled goods.

The first three do not establish the last three. Report savings only against the initial supplied quotes using the same solver. An AI-versus-deterministic negotiation comparison is still required before claiming the model itself saves more. Collect user feedback and supplier permission with their consent; never invent interviews, savings, accessibility improvements or winning odds.
