## Assessment method

Each round used three fresh Codex reviewer contexts and one frozen factual packet, without the requested score or prior subjective grades. The five user-supplied criteria were Technology, Design, Potential Impact, Innovation/Idea and Presentation. Each received an equally weighted 1–10 half-point score; totals were multiplied by 1.8 to express /90. These scale and weight choices are internal conventions, not verified official Devpost scoring. Assessments are not actual Claude API reviews, independent human judging, official scores or a guarantee of a hackathon outcome. Reviewers inspected source, evidence, captions and native stills; they did not watch the complete movie or listen to its narration. Each new round followed a material implementation or evidence change; all rounds remain retained. This is not a controlled before-and-after study.
